CONNTECHNICAL MEMORANDUM  ·  TM-CONN-01  ·  KEEPING THE WATCH OVER CONTACTS WORKING ONE MACHINE OPERATING MANUALMAN CONNGITHUB ↗

FIGURE 1-1  ·  SITUATION

1-1. Several contacts work one machine at a time: minds that take an instruction, carry what they have read, and work for an hour with no hand on them. Each has a terminal of its own, among the shells, the editors and the servers. A contact that has stopped to ask looks, from outside, like one that has not. The operator finds it by going into each, or does not.

FIGURE 1-2  ·  THE STATION

1-2. conn takes the conn of the machine. Every process is a row under the project it works, marked for what it is: ☿ a contact, Mercury, the messenger; ❯ a shell, the way in; ▯ an editor, a page held open; ◉ a service, a lamp kept lit; ○ a run, the work itself. Contacts stand first in every block.

FIGURE 1-3  ·  THE WATCH

1-3. At the end of each project’s rule the station says what the project wants of the operator, and nothing else. WAITING, blinking, where a contact has stopped to ask, and how long it has waited. A fault’s word, held still, where something ended badly. A project that wants nothing says nothing. The watch is kept by reading one line a project.

FIGURE 1-4  ·  CONTACT

1-4. tab puts the operator at the contact that has waited longest. enter puts its terminal in the workspace, the question where it was asked. The answer is given and the contact resumes its work. ctrl-space returns the keys to the panel: the operator has the conn, the contact has the work.

FIGURE 1-5  ·  STANDING DOWN

1-5. q detaches the operator. The server holds every pane; every contact carries what it has and goes on; nothing ends with the operator’s leaving. The watch resumes where it was.

SECTION 2  ·  INSTALLATION PROCEDURE

2-1. conn is a station for one operator directing many processes on one machine: a panel that keeps the watch over the processes working each project, and a workspace holding the one the operator is in, on a tmux server conn starts and holds. The contacts are Claude Code sessions; the station reads their state and shows what a waiting one asked.

$curl -fsSL https://conn.w0zro.com/install.sh | sh
