CONN MANUALGITHUB ↗

A console for the processes working your projects.

Every process on one machine, under the project it works. What each project wants of you. The one you choose, in front of you.

$curl -fsSL https://conn.w0zro.com/install.sh | sh

macOS and Linux. Needs tmux, and lsof on macOS.

The panel, the workspace, the status line.
